The Band

Overview

Role: Designer & Developer
Team: Solo project
Date: October 2024

Problem Statement

Design a simple, engaging website for a band, using animation to add energy and visual interest.

Process

I built a clean layout featuring band info and integrated a photo animation to give the site a dynamic, modern feel.

Final Design

The final site is minimal but lively, with smooth animation and a focus on showcasing the band’s image and style.
