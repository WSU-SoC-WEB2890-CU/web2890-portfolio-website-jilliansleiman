Snoopify Me

Overview

Role: Designer & Developer
Team: Solo project
Date: October 2024

Problem Statement

Create a playful, one-page personal site with a bold green theme that showcases skills and contact info in a fun, visual way.

Process

I used a bold green palette and simple layout to create a fun, one-page design highlighting my skills and contact info.

Final Design

The final site is clean, fun, and easy to navigate. It uses playful visuals and a bright color scheme to reflect personality while clearly communicating my skills and how to get in touch.
