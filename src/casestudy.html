Case Study - Bite Sized Recipes

Overview

Role: Designer & Developer
Team: Solo project
Date: October 2024

Problem Statement

Design a fun, easy-to-use recipe site for busy parents, using a bright, inviting color palette and simple layout.

Process

I researched recipe sites, sketched layout ideas, and refined the design based on user feedback and testing.

Final Designs

The final site is colorful and clean, with family-friendly visuals, clear navigation, and quick recipe access.

Research Findings & Personas

Key users included busy parents and beginner cooks, each needing quick access to simple, tasty meals.

Wireframes, Sketches & Iterations

Wireframes were tested and improved to ensure a smooth, enjoyable experience on desktop and mobile.

Final Mockups & Prototype

The working prototype features a responsive layout, recipe favorites, and a consistent visual style.

Reflection

Next time, I'd explore adding filters or meal planning tools to make the site even more helpful for families.
